# Local Authority & Citation Plan — TowingNo.1

A legitimate, white-hat plan to build local authority for a Surrey / Lower
Mainland towing business. **No** paid link schemes, PBNs, mass directory spam,
automated links, or manipulative anchors. Backlink/referring-domain data must be
pulled from a real tool (see EXTERNAL_SEO_CHECKLIST.md) — none is fabricated
here.

## 1. Foundation: Google Business Profile (highest priority)

For a service-area business, the GBP is the single biggest local ranking + trust
lever — bigger than any link.
- Claim/verify the GBP as a **service-area business** (hide the address; set the
  service-area radius across Surrey + the Lower Mainland cities).
- Primary category: **Towing service**. Secondary: Roadside assistance service.
- Complete every field: phone `(778) 838-0014`, hours (24/7), services (match the
  8 site services), service areas (match the 12 location pages), description,
  real photos of trucks/team.
- Reviews: ask every satisfied customer for a Google review (see review strategy
  below). Respond to all reviews. This is what feeds the EmbedSocial widget
  already on the homepage.

## 2. NAP citations (consistency is the goal)

Use the EXACT NAP everywhere: **TowingNo.1**, `(778) 838-0014`,
`info@towingno1.com`, `https://www.towingno1.com`, service area "Surrey &
the Lower Mainland".
- Core: Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect.
- Canada/local: Yellow Pages / YP.ca, Yelp, Better Business Bureau (if a member),
  Canada411, N49, Cylex, 411.ca.
- Automotive/towing niche: local towing/roadside directories, insurer/repair
  "approved vendor" listings where the business genuinely qualifies.
Keep a spreadsheet of every citation (URL, NAP used, status) to catch drift.

## 3. Local relationships (genuine, earned links)

- **Auto ecosystem:** local mechanics, body shops, tire shops, dealerships,
  detailers — reciprocal "who we recommend" relationships (a body shop that lists
  its preferred tow company, etc.). Real partnerships, not link swaps at scale.
- **Insurance/ICBC ecosystem:** be listed by shops in the ICBC Collision Repair
  network you work with.
- **Community:** local sponsorships (minor sports teams, community events,
  school fundraisers), Chamber of Commerce (Surrey Board of Trade), which
  typically yield a legitimate sponsor/member link + local goodwill.
- **Fleet/commercial:** property managers, strata councils, and businesses with
  parkades that need a go-to tow company.

## 4. Content-driven links (earned)

- The local + safety blog cluster (see BLOG_CONTENT_PLAN.md) is link-worthy when
  it's genuinely useful: "what to do after a crash in BC", winter-driving guides,
  highway breakdown safety. Pitch these to local news / community blogs seasonally
  (e.g., before the first snow).
- Original local data/observations (common breakdown corridors, winter call
  patterns) are the kind of first-hand content that earns citations from local
  media and is quotable by AI answer engines.

## 5. Anchor + link hygiene

- Prefer branded ("TowingNo.1") and natural/URL anchors; avoid repetitive
  exact-match ("tow truck surrey") anchors.
- Relevance and locality beat volume. A handful of real local links > hundreds of
  directory spam links.

## Explicitly avoid
Paid link networks, PBNs, mass/auto directory submissions, guest-post networks,
comment/forum spam, fake reviews or review gating, and any scheme that violates
Google's link spam / fake-engagement policies.

## Measurement
Track referring domains + local pack rankings with a real tool (GSC "Links",
Ahrefs/Semrush/Moz), plus GBP Insights for calls/direction requests. See
EXTERNAL_SEO_CHECKLIST.md.
